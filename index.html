What We Offer

Pediatric Dental Services for Every Stage of Childhood

Children’s teeth and gums change as they grow, and their dental care should reflect those needs. We provide a full range of preventive, restorative, and emergency services designed for infants, toddlers, kids, and teens.

Preventive Care & Routine Checkups

  • Comprehensive & recall exams
  • Gentle dental cleanings
  • Fluoride treatments
  • Digital X-rays (low radiation)
  • Protective sealants to prevent cavities

Restorative & Specialized Treatments

  • White fillings, Silver crowns, and White crowns
  • Pediatric root canals (pulp therapy)
  • Tooth extractions (baby & permanent)
  • Laser dentistry for painless procedures

Emergency Dental Care

  • Chipped, cracked, or knocked-out teeth
  • Painful toothaches & abscess relief
  • Sports-related dental injuries

Sedation & Special Needs Dentistry

  • IV sedation utilizing a dental anesthesia team
  • Nitrous oxide sedation (laughing gas)
  • Customized care for children with special medical or developmental needs

Meet Dr. Logan Webb

Dr. Logan Webb is a board-certified pediatric dentist dedicated to making dental visits a positive experience for every child. She specializes in preventive care, emergency treatment, sedation, and special needs dentistry, ensuring personalized care at every stage of childhood.

A Charlotte native, Dr. Webb completed her undergraduate education at Wake Forest University before earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. She completed her pediatric dentistry specialty training at the Medical University of South Carolina, where she served as chief resident. She’s a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association, and Southeastern Society of Pediatric Dentistry.

Our Technology

Advanced Pediatric Dental Technology

We use the latest technology to make dental visits more comfortable and precise, helping detect issues early and enhance your child’s experience.

iTero Digital Scanning

No more messy impressions — our digital scanner creates a 3D model of your child’s teeth for precise treatment planning, especially for orthodontic care.

Laser Dentistry

Laser technology allows for gentle, precise treatments with less discomfort, reduced anesthesia needs, and faster healing.

Low-Radiation Digital X-Rays

Our digital X-rays provide clear images with lower radiation exposure, making diagnostics safer and more effective.

With these tools, we ensure gentle, high-quality care for every child.

First Visit

What to Expect at Your Child’s First Visit

We make every child’s first visit comfortable and stress-free while giving parents the information they need for lifelong oral health.

  1. 01

    Warm Welcome

    Our friendly team will greet you, help with any paperwork, and make sure your child feels at ease.

  2. 02

    Gentle Dental Exam

    Dr. Webb will check your child’s teeth, gums, and bite to ensure everything is developing properly.

  3. 03

    Digital X-Rays (If Needed)

    For a clearer view of their oral health, we may take low-radiation digital X-rays to detect cavities or monitor growth.

  4. 04

    Professional Cleaning & Fluoride Treatment

    A gentle cleaning will remove plaque and strengthen teeth with fluoride to help prevent cavities.

  5. 05

    Parent Discussion & Oral Care Tips

    Before you leave, we’ll go over at-home care, answer any questions, and discuss any recommended treatments.
